# Example sequence (fictional)

Every company, person and number below is made up to show the shape of the output.

**Seller:** Tallyworks, an AI tool that reads maintenance logs and flags equipment likely to fail.
**Exec:** Maya Rao, founder and CEO.
**Event:** Plant Ops Summit, held at the Harbor Convention Center.
**Prospect:** Sam Ortiz, VP Operations, Bluefield Packaging (a business role in the packaging segment).

---

## Email 1

**Subject:** Sam, Plant Ops Summit: the warning in the shift notes

Sam, I noticed you'll be at Plant Ops Summit this year.

I read that Bluefield Packaging opened two plants this spring. More lines mean more shift notes, and the early signs of a failing gearbox or a slipping belt hide in them. Most surface after the line goes down.

Maya Rao, our founder and CEO, is well known in maintenance for packaging plants. Our team built Tallyworks. It reads every log with AI as it comes in, flags the equipment at risk and drafts the repair ticket. It runs on top of the maintenance system Bluefield already uses. Nothing to replace. Plants using it see a third less unplanned downtime in the first quarter.

Maya is at the Harbor Center the whole week and will make time whenever suits you. Coffee, 15 minutes? May I look at your availability on the event app and set it up?

## Email 2

**Subject:** Sam, a proof point for Bluefield

Sam, one proof point before the summit.

A regional carton maker ran Tallyworks on one quarter of shift notes. It flagged 14 failures before they happened, most on conveyor drives. Their maintenance team did not change how they work.

Maya would value your view on where plant maintenance is headed in packaging. Coffee, 15 minutes? May I look at your availability on the event app and set it up?

## Email 3

**Subject:** Bluefield at Plant Ops Summit

Sam, I noticed a few gaps in your event calendar this week.

Should I book you for coffee with Maya, our CEO? She'd love to swap notes on keeping uptime steady through rapid growth.

If it's useful, she can bring something concrete. Give us a quarter of one plant's shift notes and we'll show you which failures were visible in the logs before they happened. It takes nothing more than the files.

Just say yes and I'll set it up.

---

### What made it personal
- **Trigger:** their own news (the two new plants).
- **Use case:** the problem as it shows up in packaging (gearboxes, belts, conveyor drives).
- **Proof:** a peer in the same segment.
- **Role:** a business version. A technical contact would get the "runs on top of your maintenance system, no rebuild" angle instead.
- **Value:** a no-strings look at their own logs.
